Egyptian Civilization

contains

  • Geography and Location
    – Centered around the Nile River, which supported agriculture and trade
  • Pharaohs and Government
    – Ruled by powerful kings known as Pharaohs with a centralized government.
  • Religion and Beliefs
    – Polytheistic religion with gods like Ra, Osiris, and Isis; belief in the afterlife.
  • Pyramids and Architecture
    – Famous for monumental structures such as the Pyramids of Giza and temples.
